About Me

Hi, I’m Sean Heller, an Information Science graduate from the University of South Florida—Go Bulls! I concentrated in Web Development Technologies and am focused on UX/UI design and web development. I especially enjoy frontend work, where I can combine creativity and problem-solving to build clean, functional, and user-centered digital experiences. I’m also learning more about backend development to broaden my skills.

My Background

I’ve always enjoyed building things with my hands. Before college, I pictured myself becoming an engineer or architect, but starting college in 2020 took me in a different direction. It was a challenging time, but it also gave me the chance to discover coding. I found that I loved turning ideas into something people could use, working through problems, and seeing a project come together.

That interest led me to pursue Information Science, where I built a foundation in web development, research, information organization, and user-centered design.

What I’m Focused On

UX/UI Design

I’m developing my design process through UX coursework and a case study, from understanding user needs to organizing information and designing clear interfaces.

Frontend Development

I enjoy building responsive websites and interactive projects, then refining how they look and work. I’m also learning more about backend development to understand what supports the experience behind the scenes.

I’m continuing to strengthen both areas through hands-on projects, certifications, and portfolio work. Graduating didn’t mean I was done learning—I believe there’s always something new to explore.

Outside of Work

Outside of coding and learning, I enjoy spending time with my family and girlfriend, taking road trips, playing basketball and video games, going bowling, and listening to music. There’s a good chance I have music on while I’m working on my next project.

Let’s Connect
